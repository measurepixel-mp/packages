The rollout

Twelve months, five moves.

Weeks 1–3
Branding, website and documentary built. Conversion tracking (GA4, Tag Manager, Clarity), CRM and follow-up automation, keyword list, negative keywords and geo-fencing set up before the first rupee of ad spend.
Month 1
Google Ads goes live on phrase and exact match only, manual CPC, tight radius. The month's job is conversion data, not profit.
Months 2–3
Cut wasted spend, keep the keywords that produced calls, A/B test the landing page, refine the CRM follow-up sequences, start retargeting people who watched the documentary.
Months 4–6
Scale spend on proven keywords only. Add Performance Max now that the account has conversion history to learn from.
Months 7–12
SEO leads begin to land. Blended cost per lead falls as organic carries part of the volume paid search was carrying alone.

Service by service

What each line buys, and what it does not.

Branding

₹35,000 one time

Pros

  • One visual language across ads, website and video from day one, so every impression builds recall
  • Priced so it never delays the ads launch
  • Ends the "which logo, which colour" friction on every asset built after this

Cons

  • Essentials only: logo, palette, typography, core usage rules. No strategy workshop or collateral suite
  • A full identity upgrade comes later, at ₹1.5L to ₹2.5L, once revenue supports it
  • Fewer revision rounds than a full engagement

Our recommendation

Take the lean version now. Brand depth does not produce enquiries in month one; consistency does. Six months of ad data will tell us which message actually converts, and the full identity can then be built on evidence instead of assumptions.

Website

₹60,000 one time

Pros

  • The conversion asset: paid clicks have nowhere to convert without it
  • Built for paid search: focused landing structure, call and form tracking, fast load
  • Documentary on the hero banner pre-frames the visitor before any sales call

Cons

  • Buys a conversion-focused site, not a thirty-page content library
  • Sits on the critical path: branding finishes first, so it gates the ads launch
  • Blog and SEO page build-out belongs to the SEO line, not here

Our recommendation

Build it before the first rupee of ad spend. Sending ₹350 clicks to a weak page is how fresh accounts burn ₹60,000 with nothing to show. Minimum: hero with the documentary, trust row, project gallery, qualifier form, call and WhatsApp buttons, and a thank-you page wired to tracking.

Brand documentary

₹35,000 one time

Pros

  • Communicates the brand and its expertise in minutes, so the prospect arrives pre-framed
  • Designed to lift conversion rate on the same ad spend, lowering cost per lead with no extra budget
  • One asset, many placements: hero banner, retargeting, YouTube, sales calls, WhatsApp
  • The manufacturing unit on camera is proof competitors cannot fake with renders

Cons

  • Needs site and factory access plus one to two days of your team's time
  • Covers one shoot block at high-end production quality; extra locations or a second day cost more
  • Needs refreshing in twelve to eighteen months as projects and team change

Our recommendation

Essential, not optional. At any given budget, conversion rate is the lever we control. Moving the landing page from 2% to 3% on the same ₹60,000 is roughly two extra leads a month, which is how the documentary lowers cost per lead.

Google Ads

₹60,000 starting monthly ad budget

Pros

  • Captures people searching for interior designers in Chennai today
  • Fastest route to the first enquiry: days, not months
  • Every click is measurable, and the conversion data lowers the cost of later leads
  • ₹60,000 clears the ₹52,500 learning threshold for a fresh account at ₹350 a click

Cons

  • ₹350 a click is the high-competition reality: months one and two buy data, not profit
  • About 5 to 6 clicks a day, under the 10-a-day ideal, so the algorithm learns slower
  • A fresh account with no history can spend the month in days if targeting is loose
  • The expected lead range starts small: an estimated three to nine leads a month, not thirty

Our recommendation

Start here and hold the budget flat for ninety days. Let conversion data build under the guardrails below, then raise spend only on keywords that produced calls. Scaling before the data exists is how ad accounts lose money quietly.

SEO

₹40,000 monthly · optional

Pros

  • Brings blended cost per lead down: organic enquiries arrive without media spend
  • Compounds: results accelerate sharply after about a year and keep running
  • Competitors ranking today get free the leads we pay ₹350 a click for
  • Hedges against rising click costs, which only move one way in this category

Cons

  • Seven to eight months before meaningful results: about ₹2.8L to ₹3.2L spent before the first real return
  • No guaranteed positions: competitors and algorithm changes both move the target
  • Needs content and site structure to keep pace, so it is not a standalone service

Our recommendation

Strongly recommended, and most brands skip it for exactly the wrong reason: the wait. The clock runs only once. Starting in month one means leads in month eight; starting in month six means leads in month fourteen. If cash flow forces a delay, begin at month three once ads have proven the lead economics.

Risk controls

Protecting the first ₹60,000.

Phrase and exact match only

No broad match. It triggers ads for "interior design colleges" and "free interior design software" at full price.

Negative keywords loaded first

jobs, recruitment, course, classes, salary, ideas, wallpaper, DIY, discount, bargain — in before launch, not after.

Hyper-local geo-fencing

Not all of India, not all of Tamil Nadu. Selected high-income pin codes, or a 5 to 10 km radius around premium residential hubs.

Manual bids with a hard cap

Manual CPC or Maximise Clicks capped at ₹350 to ₹380. No uncapped Maximise Conversions until the account knows what a conversion looks like.
